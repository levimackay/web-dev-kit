# Copy

`check.sh` flags the punctuation. The rest is on whoever writes the spec.

- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere: copy, alt text, titles, comments. Avoid semicolons and mid sentence colons in prose too. They are the punctuation people now read as AI. Use periods and commas.
- No "it's not X, it's Y". No exclamation marks. No emoji. No check mark characters.
- Every fact comes from the facts file. No invented metrics, testimonials, logos, clients, review counts, years in business or certifications. Missing facts are `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` and get listed for the owner.
- Product strings match the product exactly. If the real app says "Verified by Sam Ortiz, Instructor", the page does not say "Verified by Sam Ortiz, Welding I". Check every badge, button label and screen caption against the live product.
- Claims match behaviour. Do not write "one tap" when it takes a question and a confirm. Demo counts must be right ("Waiting on you: 2" when two items are unanswered).
- Demo data is labelled as demo data, close to where it appears.
- Every line describes the reader's situation in their own words. If a line only makes sense to someone who built the product, cut it.
- Say it once. When two sections make the same point, merge them.
- The primary action reads the same everywhere it appears.
